Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof

How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
