Emergency Water Shutoff Guide in Morristown, TN

Home / Blog / Emergency Water Shutoff Guide in Morristown, TN

When water is pouring into your home, shutting it off limits the damage faster than anything else. You could avoid extensive property damage and save thousands of dollars just by knowing where your shutoff valve is and how it works. If you own a home in Morristown, TN, find your shutoff valve and practice using it now, not during a leak.

Where to Find the Main Water Shutoff

Check these spots in your Morristown home for the main valve: along an interior garage wall, close to the foundation wall down in the basement or in a crawl space, inside the laundry area or a utility room, up front where the street line enters the house, or at the meter shutoff, which is in an access box at ground level near the street. Two types are typical: gate valves, which have a round handle, and ball valves, which have a lever. Ball valves are more reliable and can be turned off with a quarter turn.

The Quick Way to Stop the Water

A gate valve closes when you turn its handle clockwise, to the right, for several full turns, to the point where it will not go further. A ball valve is off when its lever sits perpendicular to the pipe, so turn it to that position. Never force a stuck valve, since it can break and leave you worse off. Instead, use an adjustable wrench or a meter key to turn the water off at the meter.

Shutoff Valves at Each Fixture

You can usually stop water to one fixture or appliance without cutting off the whole house, because most have a shutoff valve of their own. Check behind toilets and washing machines, beneath each sink, and next to the water heater. Learn where each one is so you can quickly stop a leak in one place and still have water everywhere else in the house.
